# Engineering career ladder

| Level | Title | Typical scope |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | Engineer I | Well-defined tasks with guidance |
| E2 | Engineer II | Features end to end |
| E3 | Senior Engineer | A system or a team's roadmap |
| E4 | Staff Engineer | Several teams or a product area |
| E5 | Senior Staff Engineer | Company-wide technical direction |
| E6 | Principal Engineer | Long-term technical strategy |

Promotions to E4 and above are reviewed by the Engineering promotion committee in the March review cycle.
