# Deploy Freeze Policy

Version 2 · Effective 15 August 2025

## Weekly

No production deploys after **15:00 on Fridays**.

## Peak season

A full deploy freeze runs from **15 November to 5 January** every year, when customer warehouses run at peak volume. During the freeze, only SEV1 and SEV2 fixes may ship, with approval from the VP of Engineering.

## Exceptions

Firmware updates to customer robots always need a customer-agreed maintenance window, freeze or not.
