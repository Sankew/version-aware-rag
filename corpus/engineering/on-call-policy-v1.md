# On-call Policy

Version 1 · Effective 1 January 2024 · Owner: Platform Engineering

Engineers on the Kittiwake and robot-firmware teams join a weekly on-call rotation once they have been at Brackwater for 3 months.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a **$300 stipend** for each week on call.

## Response

Acknowledge a page within 10 minutes. If you cannot, the page escalates to the secondary on-call.

## Hand-off

Rotations change on Monday at 10:00. The outgoing engineer writes a hand-off note in the on-call channel.
