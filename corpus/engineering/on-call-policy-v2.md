# On-call Policy

Version 2 · Effective 1 November 2025 · Owner: Platform Engineering

Engineers on the Kittiwake and robot-firmware teams join a weekly on-call rotation once they have been at Brackwater for 3 months.

## Compensation

On-call engineers receive a **$400 stipend** per week on call, plus **$50 for each page** handled between midnight and 06:00.

## Response

Acknowledge a page within 10 minutes. If you cannot, the page escalates to the secondary on-call after 10 minutes and to the engineering manager after 20.

## Hand-off

Rotations change on Monday at 10:00. The outgoing engineer writes a hand-off note in the on-call channel.

## Recovery time

After a night with two or more pages, the on-call engineer may start the next day at noon.
