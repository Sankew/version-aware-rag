# Incident severity levels

| Level | Definition | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| SEV1 | Customer robots stopped at one or more sites, or a safety issue | Engineer engaged within 15 minutes, updates every 30 minutes |
| SEV2 | Major degradation (fleet throughput down more than 25%) | Engaged within 30 minutes, updates every hour |
| SEV3 | Minor degradation or a workaround exists | Next business day |
| SEV4 | Cosmetic or internal-only | Backlog |

Any SEV1 needs a post-incident review within 5 working days.
