Frequently asked questions

Accounts

How often do I need to change my password? Every 90 days. You will get a reminder email a week before it expires.

I'm locked out. Call ext. 4400. Lockouts reset automatically after 30 minutes.

Hardware

My laptop is broken. Bring it to the IT bar on the 2nd floor at Port Callan HQ, or open a ticket for a courier swap.

When will my laptop be replaced? Every 3 years. IT emails you a month before your refresh date.

Can I use my own monitor? Yes, any USB-C or HDMI monitor works with the company docks.

Software

How do I install software? Use Self Service. Anything not listed needs a ticket and a security review.
