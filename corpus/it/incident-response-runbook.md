# Security incident response runbook

## Declare

Anyone can declare a security incident in the #sec-incident channel. The on-call security engineer acknowledges within 15 minutes.

## Contain

Isolate affected hosts with the endpoint agent. Rotate any credentials that may be exposed.

## Notify

Legal decides on customer notification. Notifiable breaches of customer data are reported to affected customers within 72 hours.

## Review

A blameless post-incident review is held within 5 working days.
