# Security awareness training

All employees complete security awareness training within 5 working days of joining and once a year after that.

Key points from the 2025 course:

- Use a password of at least 14 characters; you do not need to rotate it on a schedule
- Never approve an MFA prompt you did not start
- Report phishing with the "Report" button in your mail client
- Lost device? Tell the helpdesk within 4 hours
