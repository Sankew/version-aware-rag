# VPN setup guide

1. Install the Brackwater VPN client from Self Service.
2. Sign in with your company email and approve the MFA prompt.
3. Choose the gateway closest to you: Port Callan, Ashby or Marrow Bay.

The VPN is required for the Kittiwake staging environment and the finance system. It is not required for email or chat.

If the client shows "certificate expired", restart your laptop so the device certificate renews.
