# Information Security Policy

Version 2 · Effective 1 June 2025 · Owner: Security Team

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long. Periodic password rotation is **no longer required**: change your password only if you suspect it has been exposed, or when the Security Team asks you to. The last 10 passwords cannot be reused.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is required for every company system. SMS codes are no longer accepted. Administrators of production systems must use a hardware security key.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption and the endpoint agent installed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 4 hours.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer telemetry is Confidential.
