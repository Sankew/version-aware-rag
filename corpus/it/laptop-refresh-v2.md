# Laptop Refresh Policy

Version 2 · Effective 1 October 2025

Standard laptops are replaced every **4 years**. Engineers whose work needs local builds or simulation may request a 3-year refresh with their manager's approval. IT contacts you a month before your refresh date.

Old laptops are wiped and recycled. Employees may buy their old laptop for $150 if it is at least 4 years old.
