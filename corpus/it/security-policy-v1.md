# Information Security Policy

Version 1 · Effective 1 January 2024 · Owner: Security Team

## Passwords

Passwords must be at least **12 characters** long and must be changed **every 90 days**. The last 10 passwords cannot be reused.

## Multi-factor authentication

MFA is required for email, the VPN and all production systems. Authenticator apps and SMS codes are accepted.

## Devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption and the endpoint agent installed. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer telemetry is Confidential.
