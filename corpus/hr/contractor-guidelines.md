# Contractor Guidelines

Effective 1 July 2024. Owner: People Operations and Procurement.

These guidelines apply to independent contractors and agency workers engaged by Brackwater Robotics. They do not apply to employees, whose terms are set out in the Employee Handbook.

## Time off

Contractors are not eligible for paid vacation. A contractor may take up to 10 unpaid days per contract year with the approval of their Brackwater sponsor. Contractors are not eligible for paid parental leave or paid sick days.

## Equipment

Contractors use a Brackwater-managed laptop for any work touching customer data. Personal devices may only access the contractor portal.

## Access

Building access is limited to the hours on the contractor's badge. Access to production systems needs a sponsor request and expires at the end of the contract.

## Invoicing

Invoices are due monthly and are paid within 45 days of receipt.
