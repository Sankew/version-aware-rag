# Remote Work Policy

**Effective:** 1 March 2026
**Applies to:** full-time employees
**Replaces:** the "Remote work" section of the Employee Handbook

## Office attendance

Employees are expected in their assigned office **one day a week**, on Wednesday, the company-wide anchor day. Teams may agree additional in-person days, but these are optional.

## Fully remote employees

An employee may apply to become fully remote after 12 months of service. Applications go to the employee's manager and their department head, and are reviewed each quarter. Fully remote employees attend one in-person team week per half-year, with travel paid under the Expense Policy.

## Equipment and stipends

- One-time home office stipend: $500
- Internet stipend: $40 per month, paid through payroll
- Company laptops follow the Laptop Refresh Policy regardless of where you work

## Working from another country

Working from outside your country of employment for more than 14 days in a calendar year requires approval from People Operations and Legal, because of tax and right-to-work rules.
