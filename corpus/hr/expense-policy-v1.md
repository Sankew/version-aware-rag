# Travel and Expense Policy

Version 1 · Effective 1 January 2024 · Owner: Finance

## Meals

Employees travelling on company business may claim up to **$50 per day** for meals. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Lodging

The nightly hotel cap is **$220**, including taxes. Book through the travel portal so the booking is covered by the company's duty-of-care insurance.

## Receipts

Itemised receipts are required for any expense over **$25**.

## Submitting claims

Submit expense claims in the finance system within **30 days** of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Mileage

Personal car use is reimbursed at the published mileage rate.
