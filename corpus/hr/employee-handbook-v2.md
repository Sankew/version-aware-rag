# Brackwater Robotics Employee Handbook

Version 2 · Effective 1 March 2025 · Owner: People Operations

## Welcome

Brackwater Robotics builds autonomous mobile robots for warehouses. This handbook covers the policies that apply to full-time employees of Brackwater Robotics, Inc. Contractors and interns have their own guidelines, published separately by People Operations.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, Monday to Friday. Outside core hours, teams agree their own schedules. Hourly staff in the Port Callan assembly facility follow the shift schedule posted on the floor.

## Remote work

Employees are expected in their assigned office two days a week, on days agreed with their team. Managers may approve exceptions for up to eight weeks at a time. Fully remote roles are listed as such in the job posting.

## Vacation

Full-time employees receive 25 paid vacation days per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the following year; any balance above 5 days is forfeited on 31 January. Vacation requests of more than 5 consecutive days need two weeks' notice.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave

Employees who have completed their probation period receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave, to be taken within the first year after a birth or adoption.

## Public holidays

Brackwater observes 11 paid public holidays. The annual holiday calendar is published each December.

## Probation

New employees serve a 90-day probation period. During probation, either party may end employment with one week's notice.

## Conduct

All employees must follow the Code of Conduct and the Acceptable Use Policy. Concerns can be raised with a manager, People Operations, or the anonymous ethics line.
