Benefits FAQ

Last reviewed May 2024 by People Operations.

Time off

How much vacation do I get?
Full-time employees receive 20 vacation days per year. Unused days above 5 are forfeited at the end of January.

How long is parental leave?
Eligible employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave.

Health

Who is our health insurer?
Medical, dental and vision coverage is provided through Meridian Health. Brackwater pays 90% of the employee premium and 70% for dependants.

When does coverage start?
On your first day of employment.

Retirement

Is there a retirement match?
Brackwater matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of base salary. The match vests immediately.

Other

Is there a commuter benefit?
Yes. See the Parking and Commute page.
