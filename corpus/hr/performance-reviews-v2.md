# Performance Reviews

Version 2 · Effective 1 September 2025

## Cycle

Reviews now happen twice a year, in March and September. The March cycle includes compensation decisions; the September cycle is for feedback and growth only.

## Ratings

The five-point scale is replaced by four levels: Below, Meets, Exceeds, and Outstanding.

## Pay

Salary changes from the March cycle take effect on 1 May.

## Peer feedback

Managers collect feedback from at least three colleagues, at least one from outside the employee's team.
