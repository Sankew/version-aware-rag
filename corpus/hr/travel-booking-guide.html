How to book travel

Updated August 2025.

Flights

Economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy may be booked for flights over 6 hours.

Hotels

Use the travel portal. The portal enforces the nightly cap automatically: $250 in most cities and $300 in Tier 1 cities.

Tier 1 cityCap per night
New York$300
San Francisco$300

Tokyo$300

Trains and cars

Rail is preferred for trips under 4 hours. Rental cars need manager approval.
