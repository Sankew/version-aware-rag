# Travel & Expense Policy

Version 2 · Effective 1 July 2025 · Owner: Finance

## What changed

Higher meal and lodging limits, a single receipt threshold, and a longer submission window.

## Meals

Up to **$65 per day** for meals while travelling. Alcohol is not reimbursable, including at team dinners.

## Lodging

Nightly hotel cap: **$250** including taxes, or **$300** in Tier 1 cities (see the Travel Booking Guide for the list). Book through the travel portal.

## Receipts

Itemised receipts are required for any expense over **$75**.

## Submitting claims

Submit claims within **60 days** of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

## Mileage

Personal car use is reimbursed at the published mileage rate.
