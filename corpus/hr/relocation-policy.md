# Relocation Policy

Relocation support is available when Brackwater asks an employee to move, or when a new hire accepts a role more than 80 km from their current home.

- Lump sum of $8,000 for moves within the same country, $15,000 for international moves
- Up to 30 days of temporary housing
- One house-hunting trip for the employee and a partner

If the employee leaves voluntarily within 12 months of the move, the lump sum is repaid pro rata.
