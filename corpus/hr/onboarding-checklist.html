Your first two weeks

Before day one

  • Sign your offer and complete the background check
  • Pick a laptop model (IT will email you)

Week 1

  • Collect your badge from Reception at Port Callan HQ or the Ashby office
  • Plan to be in the office three days a week from your first week
  • Complete security awareness training within 5 working days
  • Set up MFA (SMS code or authenticator app) before accessing any internal system

Week 2

  • Meet your onboarding buddy
  • Book your 30-day check-in with your manager
