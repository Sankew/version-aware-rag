# Internship Program

Owner: University Recruiting · Effective 1 January 2025

## Who this covers

Interns on 10- to 16-week summer or co-op placements.

## Pay and time off

Interns are paid hourly. Interns accrue 1 paid vacation day per full month worked, and may take unpaid days with their mentor's agreement. Interns observe the same public holidays as employees.

## Mentoring

Each intern has a mentor and a manager. Mentors meet their intern at least twice a week.

## Return offers

Return offers are decided by the end of the placement, based on the final project review.
