# Performance Reviews

Version 1 · Effective 1 January 2024

Brackwater runs one performance review cycle a year, in January. Employees write a self-review, managers collect peer feedback from at least two colleagues, and calibration happens at department level.

## Ratings

Ratings use a five-point scale from 1 (does not meet expectations) to 5 (far exceeds expectations).

## Pay

Salary changes from the review take effect on 1 April.
