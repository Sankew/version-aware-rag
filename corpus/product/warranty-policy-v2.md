# Hardware Warranty

Version 2 · Effective 1 January 2026

## Robots

Tern robots carry a **24-month** warranty on parts and labour from the date of commissioning.

## Arms

Heron arms carry an 18-month warranty.

## Batteries

Batteries are covered for 12 months or 1,500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

## Exclusions

Damage from collisions, unauthorised modification or operation outside the rated environment is not covered.
