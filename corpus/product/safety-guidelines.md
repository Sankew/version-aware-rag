# AMR site safety guidelines

- Tern robots are limited to **1.0 m/s** in zones shared with pedestrians and may run at up to 2.0 m/s in robot-only zones.
- Every Tern has two emergency stop buttons and stops within 0.5 m of a detected obstacle.
- Site supervisors must complete Brackwater safety training before operating the Site Console.
- Report any collision, near miss or e-stop failure as a SEV1 incident.
