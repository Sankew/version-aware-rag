Product FAQ

Tern AMR

What can a Tern carry? A Tern moves racks and pallets of up to 600 kg.

How long does the battery last? About 9 hours per charge, and it charges to 80% in 50 minutes.

Warranty

What warranty comes with Brackwater robots? All Brackwater robots carry a 12-month warranty on parts and labour.

Software

Do I need Kittiwake? Yes. Kittiwake is required to run a fleet of two or more robots. One Kittiwake edge server supports up to 100 robots.
