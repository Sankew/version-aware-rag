# Customer Support SLA

Version 2 · Effective 1 March 2026

| Plan | First response | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Next business day | Business hours |
| Priority | 4 hours | 24/7 |

Response times are measured from when the ticket is opened in the support portal or by phone.
