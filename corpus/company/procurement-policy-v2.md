# Procurement Policy

Version 2 · Effective 1 February 2026

| Amount | Approval |
|---|---|
| Up to $10,000 | Manager |
| $10,000 to $75,000 | Director |
| Over $75,000 | CFO |

New suppliers must pass a security review before any purchase that involves company data. Purchases over $25,000 need three quotes.
