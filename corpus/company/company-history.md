# Company history

Brackwater Robotics was founded in 2017 by Ines Okafor and Dale Brennan in a rented unit at the Port Callan docks. The first Tern prototype moved its first pallet in 2018.

- 2019: first customer site, a cold-storage warehouse in Marrow Bay
- 2021: Kittiwake 1.0 released
- 2023: Series C funding; 300 employees
- 2025: Heron picking arm launched; Ashby engineering office opened
- 2026: 640 employees and robots running at 90 customer sites

Founders' Day, 14 September, marks the day the first Tern prototype ran.
