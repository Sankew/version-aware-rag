# Parking and commute

- Port Callan HQ has free employee parking in the Harbour Road garage.
- Ashby has no on-site parking; use the Mill Lane public garage.
- The commuter benefit pays up to **$120 per month** for public transport or bike-share, pre-tax, through payroll.
- EV chargers at Port Callan are free for employees; please move your car once it is charged.
